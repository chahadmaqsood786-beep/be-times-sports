## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-performance, technical B2B manufacturing aesthetic that bridges industrial precision with elite athletic velocity. Tailored for enterprise sportswear procurement, international sportswear brands, startup apparel lines, and professional sports federations, the aesthetic conveys high-grade material engineering, rapid fulfillment, and world-class craft.

The design movement combines **High-Contrast Dark Industrialism** with **Engineered Glassmorphism**:
- Deep obsidian and pitch-black structural foundations that reflect technical machinery and premium garment dye baths.
- Sharp, luminous accent lines evoking athletic nylon taping, reflective trims, and performance telemetry.
- Precision overlays, technical registration markings, fine micro-grids, and tactile floating toolbars designed to support real-time 3D garment customization and mass-production configuration.

## Colors

The palette operates in a default dark mode to highlight high-tech fabric textures, active sportswear colors, and technical certifications.

- **Primary (`#00E5FF`)**: Electric Cyan serves as the active pulse across the interface—driving key interactions, active camera modes in 3D viewers, active state indicators, and focal metric callouts.
- **Secondary (`#2563EB`)**: Hyper Blue anchors enterprise-grade buttons, foundational B2B actions, and structural interactive states.
- **Tertiary (`#CCFF00`)**: Volt Lime is deployed deliberately for high-alert data points, instant MOQ availability alerts, urgent quotation triggers, and performance badges.
- **Neutral (`#0B0F15`)**: Deep Pitch Obsidian forms the backdrop, paired with stepped surfaces in `#0F172A` and `#1E293B` to establish visual hierarchy without heavy color noise.
- **Heritage Accents**: `#D89C33` and `#C81822` are reserved strictly for official heraldry, bespoke gold-stitch validation, and certified testing marks.

## Typography

The typography combines bold athletic condensed headlines with precise geometric body text and technical telemetry indicators:

- **Headlines (Oswald)**: Set in all-caps or structured title-case with deliberate tracking. This evokes modern performance teamwear jerseys, technical equipment labeling, and dynamic industrial strength.
- **Body & Content (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Delivers clear legibility in high-density data tables, manufacturing specification sheets, and international shipping ledgers.
- **Technical & Badge Labels (Space Grotesk)**: Monoline geometric styling used exclusively for dimensional data, ISO certifications, fabric GSM ratings, camera telemetry (e.g., `CAM_01 // 3/4 ANGLE`), and SKU trackers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout system implements a 12-column dynamic grid on desktop and tablet, collapsing to a single-column stacked hierarchy on mobile. 

- **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12 columns with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and 48px (`3rem`) canvas margins. 3D visualizer screens reserve an interactive fixed or docked 380px panel on the right with a full-bleed viewport canvas.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8 columns with 16px gutters and 24px margins. Visualizer controls collapse to floating bottom action sheets.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with 16px gutters and 20px margins. Toolbars float horizontally at the screen base above the primary checkout or quotation trigger.
- **Micro-Grid Backgrounds**: Key technical feature sections overlay an SVG coordinate grid (24px x 24px dots or fine `#1E293B` lines with 20% opacity) to accentuate the engineering environment.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses frosted glassmorphic paneling layered over dark obsidian surfaces rather than soft shadows.

- **Layer 0 (Base Canvas)**: `#0B0F15` flat background with optional fine technical vector grid.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Surface Tiers)**: `#0F172A` with a 1px solid border in `#1E293B`.
- **Layer 2 (Floating Floating Toolbars & Controls)**: Translucent `#0F172A` at 75% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a subtle `#334155` border.
- **Layer 3 (Modals, Swatch Panels, Dropdowns)**: `#1E293B` elevated with low-diffusion tinted edge glows: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.12)`.
- **Active Focus & Glow State**: Focused interactive 3D controllers and priority CTAs leverage a razor-sharp electric cyan luminescence: `0 0 12px rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.45)`.

## Shapes

The design system uses a strict **Soft (`1`)** shape language across the board, leaning into athletic precision cuts and technical equipment enclosures:
- Standard UI modules, text inputs, card containers, and panels utilize `0.25rem` (4px) corner radii.
- Medium components, floating 3D viewer viewports, and interactive drawers utilize `0.5rem` (8px).
- Status badges and technical manufacturing tags employ pill shapes or 45-degree chamfered tech corners on terminal borders to evoke laser-cut technical fabrics.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Instant Quote / Place Sample Order)**: High-velocity cyan background (`#00E5FF`) with jet black (`#0B0F15`) bold text in `Space Grotesk`. 4px radius, hover state brings a volt glow and 1px upward translateY.
- **Secondary Action (Request Catalog / Spec Sheet)**: `#1E293B` surface with `1px solid #334155`, pure white text, hover transitions border to `#00E5FF`.
- **Tertiary / Utility**: Ghost button with `text-muted` (`#94A3B8`), highlighting to Electric Cyan on hover with no background fill.

### Technical B2B Badges & Certifications
- Badges such as `Factory Direct`, `ISO 9001 Certified`, `Worldwide Air & Sea Freight`, `Private Label Ready`, and `Low MOQ 50 pcs`.
- Pill or chamfer-cornered tags with a semi-opaque background (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.8)`), 1px stroke in `#334155`, tracking at `0.1em`, featuring an electric dot status indicator (`#00E5FF` or `#CCFF00`).

### 3D Customizer & Interactive Viewer
- **Floating Camera Toolbar**: Translucent frosted bar centered at the bottom of the 3D canvas. Houses quick camera angle triggers (`Front`, `Back`, `3/4`, `Zoom-In`) with active modes backlit in `#00E5FF` at 15% fill with an accent underline.
- **Color & Texture Swatches**: Circular 32px nodes featuring athletic pantone shades, surrounded by a 2px offset border ring that illuminates in `#00E5FF` when active.
- **Configuration Tabs**: Vertical or horizontal segmented tabs (`Colors`, `Patterns`, `Logos`, `Specs`, `Quote`) set in `Space Grotesk` uppercase, bordered by sharp hairline dividers.

### Data Tables & Specification Cards
- Dark surface cards (`#0F172A`) containing technical garment specifications (GSM, stitching type, flatlock seam count, fabric composition).
- Table headers set in `label-sm` with subtle borders (`#1E293B`), data rows alternating with micro-hover highlights.

### Form Inputs & Quotation Fields
- Precision inputs with `#0B0F15` inset background, 1px border in `#334155`, and dynamic focus transitions that illuminate in `#00E5FF` without soft outline rings.
- Integrated measurement units (`pcs`, `GSM`, `inches`) displayed as monospaced label suffixes inside the input field.
