INDUSTRIAL INFRASTRUCTURE

From computerized grading to 1440 DPI Japanese sublimation rotary presses and Hashima needle detection scanners. We operate a completely integrated in-house factory floor eliminating third-party subcontractor delays.

Factory Operations

11 Industrial Manufacturing Stages

Stage 01

Fabric Greige Inspection

4-point light table inspection system testing for yarn snags, GSM consistency, tensile tear resistance, and colorfastness.

Stage 02

Automated CNC Cutting

Computer-controlled oscillating knife cutter and laser beds with ±0.5mm pattern tolerance, ensuring zero edge fraying.

Stage 03

1440 DPI Sublimation

Industrial Japanese Mimaki printing battery. Rotary continuous calender heat presses transfer artwork at 210°C with permanent chemical fiber bonding.

Stage 04

Industrial Screen Printing

Automatic carousel screen printers using eco-friendly water-based, plastisol, discharge, and high-density 3D silicone inks.

Stage 05

Direct-To-Film (DTF)

High-resolution photographic transfers on dark cotton and performance elastane blends with soft hot-melt powder adhesive backing.

Stage 06

Tajima Industrial Embroidery

20-head Japanese Tajima 15-color machines producing 3D raised foam embroidery, applique patches, and intricate team heraldry crests.

Stage 07

Precision Garment Stitching

Dedicated lines of Juki 4-needle 6-thread flatlock machines, 5-thread overlockers, and programmable bar-tack machines.

Stage 08

Dimensional Tolerance Auditing

AQL 2.5 military-standard inspection measuring chest, length, sleeve opening, and seam tension tolerance against buyer tech packs.

Stage 09

Hashima Needle Detection

100% garment piece scan through Hashima electromagnetic tunnel detectors ensuring zero broken needle fragments in exported apparel.

Stage 10

Custom Packaging & Hangtags

Custom branded matte frosted zipper bags with desiccant silica packs, branded hangtag application, and individual EAN-13 barcoding.

Stage 11

Export Carton Strapping & Cargo Dispatch

Heavy-duty 7-ply double-corrugated export master cartons with moisture barrier liners and high-tensile plastic strapping. Fast customs clearance at Sialkot Dry Port & International Airport.
