End-To-End Manufacturing Matrix

7-Stage Precision Production Sequence

Every garment flows through a meticulously monitored linear manufacturing cycle designed to eliminate defects, ensure pattern conformity, and guarantee rapid dispatch.

01 Pre-Cut

CAD Pattern Engineering & Grading

Gerber & Optitex digital nesting software systems ensure minimal fabric wastage and pinpoint sizing accuracy. Our technical patternmakers translate 2D vector technical packs into ergonomic 3D garments with graded athletic tolerances.

Optitex 3D CAD Gerber Accumark US / UK / EU / Asian Sizing < 1.2% Marker Waste
Digital Grading XS to 5XL Proportions

Proportional sleeve drop, chest arc, and collar curve retention across full grading scales.

Tech Pack Ingestion DXF & AI Native Import

Instant conversion from customer Illustrator vector flats directly into digital cut paths.

02 Cutting

Precision CNC & Laser Fabric Cutting

Automatic CNC fabric spreading tables tension and align multi-ply synthetic and natural fabrics. High-velocity CO2 laser cutting heads cut panels with 0.2mm edge accuracy while thermally sealing synthetic poly-spandex edges against fraying.

Automatic CNC Tables Dual-Head Laser Cutters Zero Edge Fraying 0.2mm Tolerance
Laser Edge Sealing Thermal Micro-Cauterization

Interlock and mesh cut borders receive instant micro-heat sealing preventing edge unraveling.

Multi-Ply Spreading Tension-Free Laying

Automated cradles lay elastane fabrics without stretching, preserving relaxed panel geometry.

03 Dye-Sub

High-Definition Sublimation Printing

Japanese Mimaki & Epson industrial wide-format dye-sub printers coupled with Italian Monti Antonio rotary heat calenders. Utilizing Italian Kiian inks to infuse vibrant 1440 DPI graphics directly into yarn fibers—guaranteed never to peel or wash out.

Mimaki TS300P-1800 Monti Antonio Calenders Italian Kiian Inks 1440 DPI Photorealistic
Color Longevity Grade 4.5+ Wash Fastness

Direct molecular bond prevents fading, running, or cracking under hot laundry wash cycles.

Breathability Integrity Zero Pore Occlusion

Unlike rubberized printing, dye-sub leaves the moisture-wicking capillary channels 100% open.

04 Embroidery

Tajima Multi-Head Industrial Embroidery

20-head Japanese Tajima computerized embroidery decks deliver dense, pinpoint crests, 3D puff badges, felt appliques, and metallic threads. Micro-tension needle controls prevent puckering on lightweight quick-dry knits.

Tajima 20-Head Stations 3D High-Density Puff Madeira German Threads 1,100 Stitches / Min
Crest Dimension Multi-Tier 3D Relief

High-density EVA foam underpinning provides rigid, raised championship-grade badges.

Backing Comfort Soft-Touch Fuse Backing

Interior embroidery backing is heat-sealed with ultra-soft tricot to protect athlete skin.

05 Assembly

Advanced Assembly & Stitching Lines

Equipped with Juki and Brother automated workstations. Our production line excels in 4-needle 6-thread flatlock stitching (ISO 607) for completely chafe-free athletic compression gear, reinforced bar-tack anchors, and bonded seamless hems.

Juki DDL-9000C Yamato 4-Needle 6-Thread Brother Electronic Bar-Tack ISO 607 Flatlock Seams
Chafe Elimination 4-Needle Interlock

Ultra-flat 0.8mm seam profile lies flush with skin, engineered specifically for MMA and running.

Structural Durability 42-Stitch Bar-Tacking

High-stress points like pocket corners, crotches, and side slits receive reinforced anchor stitches.

06 QC Lab

AQL 2.5 Quality Assurance & In-House Lab

Operating strictly under international AQL 2.5 military sampling tables. Every production batch undergoes multi-axis tensile stress analysis, Martindale pilling rub cycles, Crockmeter color fastness, and 100% electromagnetic needle scan detection.

Tensile & Tear Testing Martindale Pilling Test Shrinkage < 2.0% 100% Needle Sensor Gate
Color Fastness Standard Grade 4.5 Perspiration/UV

ISO 105-E04 verified resistant to acidic athletic sweat and intense outdoor sunlight exposure.

Zero Metal Contamination Hashima Conveyor Scanner

Sensors calibrate to 0.8mm ferrous spheres, ensuring broken needle tips never reach export pallets.

07 Packing

Finishing, Steam Tunnel Pressing & Export Packing

Garments pass through automatic vacuum pressing tables and continuous steam tunnels to eliminate micro-creases. Each unit is individually polybagged with desiccant silica, barcoded, size-stickered, and loaded into moisture-sealed export master cartons.

Continuous Steam Tunnel Desiccant Silica Pouches EAN-13 Barcode Scanning Palletized Ocean/Air Freight
Carton Compression 7-Ply Corrugated Boards

Reinforced export cartons withstand 250 lbs burst pressure, ensuring safe multi-port sea transits.

FBA & Retail Ready SKU Assortment Sorting

Pre-sorted by size, colorway, and store destination for zero-delay Amazon or retail DC check-ins.
