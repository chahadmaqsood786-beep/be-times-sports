Precision Workflow

Sportswear Manufacturing Process

01

CAD Patterning & Sizing

Digital grading across XS to 4XL. Computerized marker generation ensures maximum fabric efficiency and uniform seam dimensions.

02

1440 DPI Sublimation

Rotary roll heat presses fuse disperse sublimation dyes deep into fabric fibers at 210°C, ensuring permanent wash-proof bond.

03

4-Needle Flatlock Stitching

Juki industrial machines bind seams with high-tensile polyester core threads, eliminating skin chafing during intense athletic matches.

04

Hashima Scanner & Packing

100% individual garment scan through Hashima metal needle detectors. Custom folding, hangtag application, and carton barcode export packing.
