ENGINEERING APPAREL
FOR GLOBAL MARKETS

Headquartered in the historic sports manufacturing center of Sialkot, Pakistan, GORSY is an integrated OEM apparel and sports equipment manufacturing partner for athletic clubs, institutional schoolwear suppliers, and independent private-label brands worldwide.

By unifying digital CAD patternmaking, high-speed 1440 DPI Japanese sublimation printing, Tajima multi-head embroidery, and automated cutting under one roof, we eliminate intermediate contracting layers and deliver verifiable quality with direct Sialkot factory wholesale pricing.

Corporate Governance

Manufacturing Values & Ethics

verified_user

Zero Child-Labor & Fair Wages

Strict compliance with the International Labour Organization (ILO) conventions. We strictly prohibit child labor and guarantee fair living wages, healthcare coverage, and safe ergonomic working environments for all factory artisans.

eco

Eco-Friendly Dye Chemistry

Our sublimation and screen printing departments utilize water-based, OEKO-TEX certified European and Japanese non-toxic disperse dyes. Free of heavy metals, formaldehyde, and harmful alkylphenol ethoxylates (APEOs).

biotech

AQL 2.5 Quality Assurance

Multi-stage physical inspection protocol: raw yarn tensile audit, post-sublimation colorfastness rubbing tests, seam pull testing up to 25 kg force, and 100% individual garment scan through Hashima tunnel needle detectors.
